# Finger Drag

Adds direct finger dragging to the Geometry Dash editor on Android.

## How it works

- Open a level in the editor.
- Touch a visible level object.
- Keep your finger down and move it.
- The selected object follows the finger.
- Release the finger to leave the object at its new position.

This version targets Geometry Dash **2.2081** and Geode **5.10.1**.
